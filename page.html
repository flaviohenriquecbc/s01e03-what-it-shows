Not known. Two of the three problems have no ticket of their own, and a fix for the parser will not close them.

1. The parser flips day and month

The shared parser reads every date month first. All 11 tenants write the day first, so app and email reports come out flipped. This is the bug #4471 describes.

2. The booking-v3 form hint says MM/DD

Alder Physio, Pale Harbour and Copper Studio use booking-v3, which prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field. Their people type month first, and the form reads it day first. Fixing the parser does not change this. Intake tagged about half of these reports parser, which is wrong.

3. Some bookings move by one day

Five reports describe a one-day move, not a flip: R-41202, R-41209, R-41236, R-41344 and R-41620. The clues are "only June", an invite that arrived before the booking, and one meeting that showed as Tuesday in Lisbon and Monday in Toronto. This points to a time zone or daylight-saving bug. It is a guess from the reports and needs engineering to confirm.

What to do

  • Keep #4471 for problem 1 only.
  • Open a ticket for problem 2 and one for problem 3.
  • Do not merge on subject line alone (decision 0001): it hid these two problems inside #4471.
